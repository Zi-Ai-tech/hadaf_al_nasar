Batch Record Income

Critical Operation

Warning

This operation will create income records for ALL paid invoices that don't have income recorded.

Important Information
  • Income will be recorded only for fully paid invoices
  • Each invoice will only get one income record
  • This operation cannot be undone
  • The process may take several minutes for large datasets
What will happen?
  • System will find all paid invoices without income records
  • For each invoice, create an income record with full amount
  • Update each invoice's income_created flag to True
  • Show detailed results upon completion
Estimated Impact

This will affect all invoices that are:

  • Marked as "paid" or have full payment
  • Have income_created = False
  • Have total_amount > 0

The exact number will be calculated during processing.
